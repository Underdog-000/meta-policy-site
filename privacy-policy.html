Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Facebook Page Support Assistant collects, uses, and protects information when users interact with connected Facebook Pages.

Information we process

When a user comments on or messages a connected Facebook Page, we may process information provided by Meta, such as comment text, message text, comment ID, page ID, post ID, sender ID, timestamps, and related interaction data.

How we use information

We use this information to help page administrators manage customer support interactions, respond to comments, send relevant private replies, prevent duplicate processing, and maintain basic operational logs.

Data storage

Interaction data may be stored in connected workflow tools or databases used by the page administrator for support and automation purposes.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by service providers used to operate the automation workflow, such as hosting, database, workflow automation, and AI processing providers.

Data retention

We retain data only as long as reasonably necessary for support, security, debugging, fraud prevention, analytics, or legal compliance.

User rights

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their data by contacting us at fmjxieoz@lamesamail.com.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact: fmjxieoz@lamesamail.com
