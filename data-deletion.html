Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 5, 2026

This page explains how users can request deletion of data related to Facebook Page Support Assistant.

How to remove app access from Facebook

You can remove app access from your Facebook account by going to:

Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites → find the connected app → Remove.

How to request deletion of stored data

To request deletion of data stored by us, send an email to:

fmjxieoz@lamesamail.com

Use the subject line: “Data Deletion Request”.

Please include the following information:

  • Your full name
  • Your Facebook profile link or Facebook user ID, if available
  • The Facebook Page or comment/message related to your request
  • The email address we can use to contact you

Processing

We will review your request and delete or anonymize applicable stored data unless we are required to retain it for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.

Contact

Email: fmjxieoz@lamesamail.com
